# Customer Success Interview Guide
## TaxBridge - Paid User Interview Framework

### Objective
Interview 5+ paid users to understand:
1. What problem did we solve?
2. What almost made them not buy?
3. What feature would make them refer friends?

### Interview Setup

**Target:** 5-10 paid users (Pro or Enterprise subscribers)
**Duration:** 15-20 minutes per interview
**Format:** Video call (Zoom/Google Meet) or email survey
**Incentive:** $25 Amazon gift card or 2 months free subscription

### Selection Criteria

**Ideal candidates:**
- ✅ Active paid subscribers (Pro/Enterprise)
- ✅ Completed 3+ calculations
- ✅ Subscribed for 14+ days (past initial excitement phase)
- ✅ Mix of engagement levels (high, medium, churned)
- ✅ Different user segments: H-1B vs TN visa, different companies, different states/provinces

**Avoid:**
- ❌ Free users only (different mindset)
- ❌ Subscribers < 7 days (too new, haven't formed opinions)
- ❌ Friends/family testers (biased feedback)

### Interview Script

---

#### Opening (2 minutes)

> "Hi [Name]! Thanks for taking the time to chat with me. I'm Michael, founder of TaxBridge.
>
> I'm talking to a handful of our paid customers to understand what's working, what's not, and how we can make TaxBridge even better for cross-border tax filers like yourself.
>
> This is super informal - I'm just here to learn from you. Everything you say is confidential and will help shape our roadmap. Sound good?"

**Build rapport:**
- Where are you based? (Seattle, Vancouver, Toronto, etc.)
- What company? H-1B or TN visa?
- How long have you been dealing with cross-border taxes?

---

#### SECTION 1: The Problem (5 minutes)

**Q1: What problem did we solve for you?**

*Probing questions:*
- What were you doing before TaxBridge?
- How were you handling cross-border taxes?
- What was frustrating about your old process?
- How much time/money were you spending on taxes before?

**Listen for:**
- 🎯 **Pain points** (time, money, complexity, CPA costs, errors)
- 💰 **Quantifiable impact** ("Saved $X", "Saved Y hours")
- 😤 **Emotional language** ("frustrating", "confusing", "scary", "relieved")
- 🔑 **Core value props** (what they emphasize most)

**Example answers to guide them:**
- "I was paying a CPA $2,000/year and still didn't understand my taxes"
- "I spent 15+ hours on Excel trying to calculate FTC myself"
- "I was terrified I was doing my T1135 wrong and would get audited"
- "I didn't know I could claim FTC on RSU income until TaxBridge showed me"

---

#### SECTION 2: Purchase Barriers (4 minutes)

**Q2: What almost made you NOT buy TaxBridge?**

*Probing questions:*
- What made you hesitate before subscribing?
- What concerns did you have?
- What would have convinced you faster?
- Did you compare us to any alternatives?

**Listen for:**
- 🚧 **Objections** (price, trust, "will this work for my situation?")
- 🤔 **Hesitation points** (lack of social proof, unclear value, complex pricing)
- 🔍 **Decision criteria** (what they researched, what convinced them)
- 💭 **Competitive alternatives** (CPAs, TurboTax, DIY Excel)

**Example answers to guide them:**
- "I wasn't sure if it would work for my specific state/province combo"
- "I needed to see more reviews/testimonials first"
- "I was worried it would be too complicated to use"
- "I almost went with a CPA instead because it felt 'safer'"
- "$49/year felt expensive until I compared it to a $2,000 CPA bill"

---

#### SECTION 3: Referral Triggers (5 minutes)

**Q3: What feature or outcome would make you actively refer TaxBridge to friends?**

*Probing questions:*
- What would make you message your coworkers about this?
- What would make you post about it on Blind or Reddit?
- What success would make you want to share it?
- Who do you know who needs this?

**Listen for:**
- 🎁 **Referral motivators** (reward, altruism, status, reciprocity)
- 📣 **Shareable outcomes** (money saved, time saved, anxiety relief)
- 👥 **Target audience** (who they'd refer to: coworkers, friends, Reddit communities)
- 💡 **Feature gaps** (what's missing that would make them champions)

**Example answers to guide them:**
- "If I save $5K+ in taxes, I'd definitely tell my H-1B friends"
- "If there was a referral bonus, I'd share it in my company Slack"
- "If TaxBridge auto-filled my 1116 form perfectly, I'd post about it on Reddit"
- "I'd refer friends if there was a 'share my results' feature (anonymized)"

---

#### SECTION 4: Deep Dive (3 minutes)

**Q4: If you could wave a magic wand and add ONE feature to TaxBridge, what would it be?**

**Q5: What's the most valuable thing TaxBridge does for you?**
- Calculator accuracy?
- Multi-year planning?
- Forms checklist?
- Time saved?
- Peace of mind?

**Q6: Have you recommended TaxBridge to anyone yet? Why or why not?**

---

#### Closing (1 minute)

> "This has been incredibly helpful - thank you! A few final questions:
>
> 1. Can we use your feedback as a testimonial (anonymized if you prefer)?
> 2. Would you be open to a quick follow-up call in 3-6 months?
> 3. Is there anything else you think I should know?"

**Send thank you:**
- Email $25 Amazon gift card OR credit 2 months free
- Thank them for their time
- Tell them when to expect product updates based on their feedback

## Analysis Framework

### After 5 Interviews: Pattern Analysis

#### 1. Problem Clustering
Group all "problems we solved" into themes:
- **Time savings** (how many mentioned? average hours saved?)
- **Money savings** (how many mentioned? average $ saved?)
- **Complexity reduction** ("I finally understand FTC")
- **Peace of mind** ("No longer scared of audit")
- **CPA replacement** ("Saved $X on CPA fees")

**Output:** Top 3 problems we solve (ranked by frequency)

#### 2. Barrier Analysis
Identify the top 3 purchase objections:
- Price too high?
- Lack of trust/social proof?
- Unclear value proposition?
- Complexity concerns?
- "Will this work for MY situation?"

**Output:** Top 3 barriers + how we overcame them

#### 3. Referral Messaging
Extract the top 3 referral triggers:
- Shareable outcomes ("I saved $5K!")
- Feature completeness ("Auto-fills 1116")
- Referral incentive ("Get 2 months free")
- Altruism ("Help your friends avoid tax headaches")

**Output:** 3 referral program taglines based on user language
