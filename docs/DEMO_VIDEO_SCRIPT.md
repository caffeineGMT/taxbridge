# TaxBridge Demo Video Script
**Duration**: 60-90 seconds
**Format**: Screen recording with voiceover
**Audience**: Product Hunt viewers (tech-savvy, short attention span)

---

## Video Structure

### HOOK (0-5 seconds)
**Screen**: Landing page hero section
**Voiceover**: "I overpaid $12,000 in taxes on my Meta stock because I didn't understand cross-border tax rules."

**Visual**: Highlight hero headline with mouse cursor pointing to it

---

### PROBLEM (5-15 seconds)
**Screen**: Scroll down to problem section or show calculator intro
**Voiceover**: "If you've worked in the US on a visa and moved to Canada, both countries want to tax your RSU income. Most people either hire a $2,000-per-year accountant or overpay by thousands."

**Visual**: Show problem visuals - two flags (US/Canada), tax forms, money flying away

---

### SOLUTION PART 1: Calculator (15-30 seconds)
**Screen**: Navigate to tax calculator (`/tax-calculator/h1b-worker-canada`)
**Voiceover**: "TaxBridge calculates your exact tax liability in both countries. Just enter your RSU vesting details - date, shares, fair market value, employer."

**Visual Actions**:
1. Click "Try Calculator" button
2. Select employer from dropdown (e.g., "Meta")
3. Enter vesting date (e.g., "2024-02-15")
4. Enter shares (e.g., "100")
5. Enter FMV (e.g., "$450.00")
6. Click "Calculate Taxes"

---

### SOLUTION PART 2: Results (30-45 seconds)
**Screen**: Tax calculation results
**Voiceover**: "The calculator shows your US federal, state, Canada federal, and provincial taxes side-by-side. It automatically applies the US-Canada tax treaty Article XV to calculate your Foreign Tax Credit, so you only pay tax once."

**Visual Actions**:
1. Scroll through tax breakdown showing:
   - US Federal Tax: $15,400
   - California State Tax: $5,200
   - Canada Federal Tax: $11,300
   - BC Provincial Tax: $3,800
   - **Foreign Tax Credit: -$14,600**
   - **Effective Total: $21,100** (vs $35,700 without FTC)
2. Highlight savings: "$14,600 saved by FTC"

---

### SOLUTION PART 3: Forms & Dashboard (45-60 seconds)
**Screen**: Forms checklist and dashboard
**Voiceover**: "You also get a checklist of every form you need to file - W-2, 1040, T1, FBAR, Form 8938 - and a multi-year dashboard to track all your vesting events."

**Visual Actions**:
1. Quick cut to forms checklist page
2. Show checked/unchecked boxes
3. Quick cut to dashboard with multiple RSU entries
4. Show multi-year chart with trends

---

### PRICING/CTA (60-75 seconds)
**Screen**: Pricing page
**Voiceover**: "Free tier for basic calculations. Pro is $299 per year for unlimited RSUs, multi-year tracking, and PDF exports."

**Visual Actions**:
1. Scroll through pricing tiers
2. Highlight Pro tier ($299/year)
3. Show feature checkmarks

---

### CLOSING (75-90 seconds)
**Screen**: Back to landing page with URL visible
**Voiceover**: "If you're a Canadian tech worker with US stock grants, try TaxBridge at taxbridge.app and stop overpaying taxes."

**Visual**:
1. Show URL clearly: **taxbridge.app**
2. Optional: Add text overlay "Save $5K-$15K per year"
3. Fade to Product Hunt logo or end card

## Recording Checklist

### Pre-Recording Setup

**Browser Preparation**:
- [ ] Use Chrome in Incognito mode (no extensions, clean UI)
- [ ] Set browser zoom to 100%
- [ ] Clear browser cookies/cache
- [ ] Window size: 1920x1080 (Full HD)
- [ ] Hide bookmarks bar (Cmd+Shift+B on Mac)

**Sample Data Ready**:
- [ ] Employer: Meta Platforms, Inc.
- [ ] Vesting Date: February 15, 2024
- [ ] Shares: 100
- [ ] Fair Market Value: $450.00
- [ ] State: California
- [ ] Province: British Columbia

**Environment**:
- [ ] Close all other apps (clean desktop)
- [ ] Disable notifications (macOS: Do Not Disturb)
- [ ] Test microphone (clear audio, no background noise)
- [ ] Good lighting (if showing face/webcam)

### Recording Tools

**Recommended: Loom** (Easiest)
- Free tier: 5-minute videos
- Website: loom.com
- Benefits: Auto-upload, easy sharing, built-in trimming
- Export as MP4 for Product Hunt

**Alternative: OBS Studio** (Professional, Free)
- Download: obsproject.com
- Settings:
  - Canvas: 1920x1080
  - Output: 1080p, 30fps
  - Encoder: H.264
  - Bitrate: 5000 kbps
- Benefits: Full control, local recording, no watermark

**Mac Built-in: QuickTime**
- File > New Screen Recording
- Select microphone
- Click record, select area
- Benefits: No installation, simple
- Downside: Minimal editing

### Recording Session

**Dry Run**:
1. Practice the script 2-3 times without recording
2. Time yourself (aim for 75-85 seconds)
3. Smooth out any awkward transitions
4. Ensure data entry is quick (practice typing)

**Actual Recording**:
1. Start recording tool
2. Navigate to landing page
3. Take a deep breath
4. Start voiceover with hook
5. Follow script exactly
6. Move cursor deliberately (not too fast)
7. Pause briefly between sections
8. End on clear CTA

**If You Make a Mistake**:
- Don't stop! Keep recording
- Pause for 2 seconds
- Re-do just that section
- You can edit it out later

### Post-Production

**Editing** (Optional but Recommended):
- Trim dead space at start/end
- Cut out any mistakes
- Add zoom-ins on important sections (FTC savings, pricing)
- Speed up repetitive typing (1.5x-2x speed)
- Add text overlays for key points:
  - "Save $14,600 with FTC"
  - "Free tier available"
  - "taxbridge.app"

**Captions/Subtitles** (Highly Recommended):
- Many Product Hunt users watch muted
- Tools:
  - Kapwing (kapwing.com/tools/add-subtitles)
  - Rev.com (paid, $1.50/minute)
  - YouTube auto-captions (free but requires export)
- Style: White text, black background, sans-serif font

**Background Music** (Optional):
- Use ONLY royalty-free music
- Keep volume low (voiceover is primary)
- Sources:
  - Epidemic Sound (paid, high quality)
  - YouTube Audio Library (free)
  - Uppbeat (free with attribution)
- Genre: Upbeat, tech, corporate (nothing distracting)

**Export Settings**:
- Format: MP4 (H.264)
- Resolution: 1920x1080 (Full HD)
- Frame Rate: 30 fps
- Bitrate: 5,000-8,000 kbps
- File size: Under 100 MB (Product Hunt limit)

### Upload Options

**Option 1: Direct to Product Hunt**
- Upload during submission
- Max 100 MB
- Product Hunt hosts it

**Option 2: YouTube (Unlisted)**
- Upload to YouTube
- Set to "Unlisted" (not public, not private)
- Paste YouTube link in Product Hunt submission
- Benefits: No size limit, better video player

**Option 3: Vimeo**
- Upload to Vimeo
- Set to "Anyone with link can view"
- Paste link in Product Hunt
- Benefits: Professional, no ads

## Voiceover Tips

**Tone**:
- Conversational, not sales-y
- Confident but humble
- Authentic (you experienced this problem)

**Pacing**:
- Speak clearly and slowly
- Pause between sentences
- Don't rush - silence is OK

**Energy**:
- Start strong (hook grabs attention)
- Build enthusiasm through solution
- End with confidence (CTA)

**Common Mistakes to Avoid**:
- Talking too fast (most common mistake)
- Monotone voice (inject emotion)
- Too many "um"s and "uh"s (practice helps)
- Reading script robotically (sound natural)

## Alternative: No Voiceover (Text + Music Only)

If you're not comfortable with voiceover:

1. Record screen demo (no audio)
2. Add text overlays with key points
3. Add upbeat background music
4. Use Product Hunt examples:
   - Notion (text-heavy demo)
   - Superhuman (fast cuts with text)

Benefits:
- Easier to record
- No voice quality issues
- Works in any language

Downsides:
- Less personal
- Harder to tell founder story
- Lower engagement

## Video Length Guidelines

**Ideal**: 60-75 seconds
- Product Hunt users have short attention spans
- Shows you respect their time
- Forces you to focus on core value

**Maximum**: 90 seconds
- After 90s, drop-off increases significantly
- If you can't explain it in 90s, simplify

**Minimum**: 45 seconds
- Too short = not enough context
- Under 30s feels incomplete
