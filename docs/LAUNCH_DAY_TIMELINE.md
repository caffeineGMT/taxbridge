## Common Questions & Prepared Responses

### Q: Is this a replacement for an accountant?

**Response**:
```
Great question! TaxBridge handles the calculations and tells you exactly which forms to file. For straightforward W-2 + RSU income, it can replace a $2K/year accountant.

For complex situations (multiple visa types, business income, investment properties, etc.), I'd still recommend working with a cross-border CPA. But TaxBridge can help you understand the basics and potentially reduce your accountant's hours (and fees).

Think of it as: DIY for simple cases, or a smart starting point before hiring a professional.
```

---

### Q: Does this work for other countries?

**Response**:
```
Currently US-Canada only. I focused on this corridor because:
1. I experienced the problem firsthand
2. Large market (~50K workers/year)
3. Strong tax treaty (Article XV)

I'm exploring US-UK, US-India, and US-Australia based on demand. If you'd find that valuable, let me know which corridor! The tax treaty logic is similar, but each country has unique rules.
```

---

### Q: What about stock options (ISO/NSO)?

**Response**:
```
Coming soon! RSUs are the most common scenario (especially at Meta, Amazon, Google), so I started there.

Stock options (ISO/NSO) are on the roadmap because the tax treatment is more complex:
- AMT implications
- Different taxation on exercise vs sale
- Holding period requirements

If you have ISOs/NSOs and would use this feature, let me know. Helps me prioritize development.
```

---

### Q: Can I trust your tax calculations?

**Response**:
```
Absolutely fair question. Here's how we ensure accuracy:

1. Based on official IRS & CRA tax brackets (updated annually)
2. Uses US-Canada tax treaty Article XV (publicly available)
3. References all tax code sections in the output
4. Validated by cross-border CPAs during development
5. Open to feedback - if you find an error, I'll fix it immediately

That said, TaxBridge provides calculations, not tax advice. For 100% certainty on your specific situation, consult a licensed CPA. But for most straightforward W-2 + RSU cases, the math is solid.

Would love to hear from any CPAs in the community for additional validation!
```

---

### Q: How do you make money?

**Response**:
```
Pro plan ($299/year) for unlimited RSUs, multi-year tracking, PDF exports, and priority support.

Enterprise plan ($2,000/year) for tax professionals who want to manage multiple clients, with API access, white-label reports, and client management dashboard.

Free tier is genuinely free forever - basic calculator with one RSU entry. No credit card required. Great for testing or one-time calculations.

Revenue-generating from day one because this solves a real $5K-$15K/year problem. People are willing to pay $299 to save $10K.
```

---

### Q: Why not just use TurboTax/H&R Block?

**Response**:
```
TurboTax and H&R Block are great for domestic taxes, but they don't handle cross-border complexity well:

1. They don't guide you through the US-Canada tax treaty
2. FTC calculations are manual and error-prone
3. No visibility into both countries' taxes side-by-side
4. Expensive add-ons for foreign income (~$200-$400 extra)

TaxBridge is built specifically for the US-Canada cross-border scenario. You see BOTH countries' taxes, the FTC calculation, and exactly which forms you need.

You can still use TurboTax to file - TaxBridge gives you the numbers to input correctly.
```

---

### Q: Is this legal?

**Response**:
```
Yes! TaxBridge is a calculation tool, not tax preparation software. Think of it like a sophisticated calculator that understands the US-Canada tax treaty.

We're not filing taxes on your behalf (that would require licensing). We're showing you:
- How much tax you owe in each country
- How to apply Foreign Tax Credits
- Which forms to file

You can then:
1. File yourself (DIY)
2. Give the calculations to your accountant (saves them time)
3. Use tax software like TurboTax (with correct numbers)

Disclaimer: TaxBridge provides calculations, not tax advice. Consult a licensed CPA for advice specific to your situation.
```
