The day I got a $12,000 tax surprise changed my career. 💸

In 2024, I moved from Meta's Menlo Park office to Vancouver on a work visa. I had 200 unvested RSUs worth ~$90K.

When they vested after my move, I owed taxes in BOTH countries.

I paid a cross-border CPA $3,000 to file my returns. When I got the bill, I was shocked:

US Federal: $30,800
CA State: $10,400
Canada Federal: $22,600
BC Provincial: $7,600

Total: $71,400 on $90K income (79% effective tax rate!!!)

Something felt wrong.

I spent 3 weeks reading IRS Publication 54, CRA Guide T4055, and the US-Canada Tax Treaty (Article XV). Turns out my CPA missed the Foreign Tax Credit optimization.

Correct calculation:
• US Total: $41,200
• Canada Total: $30,200
• FTC Applied: -$30,200
• Actual Tax Owed: $41,200

I overpaid by $30,200. 😱

1. Most CPAs don't specialize in cross-border
