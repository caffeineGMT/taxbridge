## Outreach Execution Plan

### Timing

**7 days before launch** (e.g., April 1 for April 8 launch):
- Send first DM to all 10 hunters
- Prioritize Tier 1 (Chris, Ryan, Kevin)
- Space out DMs by 1 hour to avoid spam flags

**4 days before launch** (e.g., April 4):
- Send follow-up DM to non-responders
- Focus on Tier 2 if Tier 1 didn't respond
- Adjust messaging based on any early feedback

**2 days before launch** (e.g., April 6):
- Confirm with whoever responded
- Send Product Hunt preview link
- Share maker comment draft for alignment
- Provide launch time (Tuesday 12:01am PST)

**Launch day minus 1** (e.g., April 7):
- Final confirmation with hunter
- Share demo video if ready
- Confirm they can post at 12:01am PST sharp

### Success Metrics

**Primary goal**: 1 confirmed hunter
**Backup plan**: Self-hunt if no hunter responds by 3 days before launch
**Ideal outcome**: 2-3 hunters interested (choose best fit)
