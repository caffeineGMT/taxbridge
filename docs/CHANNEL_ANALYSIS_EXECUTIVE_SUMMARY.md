## ✅ DECISION RATIONALE

### Why NOT Product Hunt?
- One-time traffic spike (500-1,000 visitors)
- Expected 7-15 customers = $60-120 MRR
- Not sustainable (launch happens once)
- Better as SECONDARY channel after SEO is working

### Why NOT Paid Ads?
- CPC: $5-15/click
- Budget needed: $1,500-3,000/month
- ROI: NEGATIVE at $49/year pricing ($150-450 CAC vs $49 LTV)
- Not viable until pricing increases or LTV improves

### Why NOT Just Optimize Landing Page?
- A/B tests can improve conversion +15-35%
- BUT need traffic first (can't optimize 0 visitors)
- Landing page optimization is DEPENDENT on traffic channels
- Should run in PARALLEL with SEO, not instead of
