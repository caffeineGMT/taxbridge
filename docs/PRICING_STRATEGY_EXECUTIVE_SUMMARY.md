## ⚡ Key Risks & Mitigations

### Risk 1: "Low price = low quality" perception
**Mitigation:**
- Premium branding (professional design)
- Money-back guarantee: "100% accurate or full refund"
- Social proof: "100+ H1B professionals trust TaxBridge"

### Risk 2: Hard to raise prices later
**Mitigation:**
- Grandfather existing users at $29 "forever" (loyalty)
- New users pay $49-$79 (segment by acquisition date)
- Premium add-ons: CPA review ($99), audit defense ($49)

### Risk 3: Insufficient revenue
**Mitigation:**
- Break-even: 100 users × $29 = $2,900 ARR (achievable Month 6)
- Enterprise upsell: Immigration lawyers $500/year bulk licenses
- Add-ons expand margin: Expert review, multi-year planning
