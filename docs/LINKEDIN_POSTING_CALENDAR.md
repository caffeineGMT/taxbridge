## Content Formats

### Format 1: Carousel Posts (Most Engaging)
**Structure**:
- Slide 1: Hook + problem statement
- Slides 2-7: Key insights / steps
- Slide 8: CTA (link to full article)

**Design**:
- Use Canva Pro template (brand colors: #0066CC primary, #00BFFF secondary)
- Consistent font: Inter Bold for headings, Inter Regular for body
- Icons: Streamline or Feather icon sets
- White background with color accents

**Tools**: Canva Pro, Figma (for custom designs)

### Format 2: Data Visualizations
**Chart Types**:
- Bar charts: Tax rate comparisons
- Line charts: Tax over time, income scenarios
- Tables: Side-by-side comparisons (TN vs H1B, RRSP vs 401k)
- Flowcharts: Decision trees (should I file 83(b)?)

**Tools**: Datawrapper, Flourish, Canva

### Format 3: Infographics
**Use Cases**:
- Process diagrams (how to file FBAR)
- Checklist visuals (tax filing timeline)
- Comparison matrices (FBAR vs Form 8938)

**Tools**: Venngage, Canva, Adobe Illustrator

### Format 4: Video (30-60 seconds)
**Topics**:
- Screen recordings: Calculator walkthrough
- Talking head: Quick tax tips
- Animated explainers: FTC concept

**Tools**: Loom (screen recording), Canva Video, Adobe Premiere Rush

## Engagement Best Practices

1. **Post Timing**:
   - Best times: Tuesday-Thursday, 8am-10am ET
   - Avoid: Mondays (inbox clearing), Fridays after 2pm (weekend mode)

2. **Reply to Comments**:
   - Reply within 2 hours to ALL comments (algorithm boost)
   - Ask follow-up questions to encourage discussion
   - Tag relevant connections if they can help

3. **Engage Before Posting**:
   - Spend 10 minutes engaging with others' content BEFORE you post
   - Comment on 5-10 posts in your niche
   - Algorithm favors active users

4. **Cross-Promote**:
   - Share LinkedIn posts to Twitter/X with shortened link
   - Embed top LinkedIn posts in blog articles
   - Reference LinkedIn discussions in email newsletters

## Tracking & Optimization

### Metrics to Track (Weekly)
- **Impressions**: How many people saw the post
- **Engagement rate**: (Likes + Comments + Shares) / Impressions
- **Click-through rate**: Clicks on article link / Impressions
- **Profile views**: New profile visits from posts
- **Follower growth**: Net new followers per week

### Tools
- **LinkedIn Analytics**: Built-in metrics dashboard
- **Google Analytics**: Track traffic from linkedin.com
- **PostHog**: User behavior from LinkedIn referrals

### Optimization
- **A/B test post formats**: Carousel vs single image vs text-only
- **A/B test hooks**: Question vs stat vs story
- **Double down on winners**: Repost top performers after 30 days
