## Email Service Setup

### Option 1: SendGrid (Recommended)

**Setup:**
1. Create SendGrid account (free tier: 100 emails/day)
2. Verify sender domain (taxbridgecalc.com)
3. Create email template with dynamic fields: {{first_name}}, {{savings_amount}}, {{product_hunt_link}}
4. Upload email list CSV with segments
5. Schedule sends for Monday 8 AM, 10 AM, 2 PM, 6 PM PST
6. Schedule reminder for Tuesday 6 AM PST

**Tracking:**
- Open rate: Target 60%+
- Click rate: Target 40%+
- Upvote conversion: Track via Product Hunt analytics

### Option 2: Mailchimp (Alternative)

**Setup:**
1. Create Mailchimp account (free tier: 500 contacts)
2. Import contacts with tags: power_user, trial_user, newsletter, personal
3. Create 4 campaigns (one per segment)
4. Schedule automation for Monday/Tuesday sends
5. Track opens, clicks, upvotes

### Option 3: Manual Gmail (For Small Lists)

**Setup:**
1. Draft 4 email templates in Gmail
2. Use BCC for sending (max 50 recipients per email)
3. Personalize first line for each recipient
4. Send manually at scheduled times
5. Track responses manually

### For Power Users:
- Reference their specific savings: "You saved $9,200 using TaxBridge"
- Mention their usage: "You've calculated 3 RSU vesting events"
- Ask for testimonial: "Would love if you shared your experience in a PH comment"

### For Trial Users:

- Remind them of pain point: "Avoid overpaying like most cross-border filers"

- Keep it educational: "Here's what TaxBridge does..."
- Lower ask: "Just an upvote helps, even if you don't need tax software"
