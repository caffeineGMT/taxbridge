## 💰 Cost Comparison (What Users Actually Pay)

| User Scenario | TaxBridge | Alternatives | Savings |
|---------------|-----------|--------------|---------|
| H-1B with RSUs (single state) | **$79** | TurboTax $149 + Wealthsimple $40 = **$189** | **58% cheaper** ($110 saved) |
| TN with RSUs (multi-state) | **$79** | TurboTax $149 + $49 state + Wealthsimple $40 = **$238** | **67% cheaper** ($159 saved) |
| CPA with 20 clients | **$2,000** ($100/client) | Manual prep 3hrs × $150 × 20 = **$9,000** | **78% cheaper** ($7,000 saved) |

**Key Finding:** Users save $110-$159 (58-67%) by using TaxBridge instead of buying two separate tax software products.

---

## ✅ Why Our Pricing Works

1. **Aligned with specialists:** $49 matches Glacier Tax ($49 for US-only)
2. **Below premium brands:** TurboTax Premier charges $149 for US-only
3. **Justified premium:** Dual-country + FTC optimization = 2x value of single-country tools
4. **Clear ROI:** Saves users $110-$159 vs buying TurboTax + Wealthsimple
5. **Psychological anchoring:** $79 feels cheap compared to $189 alternative

## 🔴 DO NOT Do These

1. ❌ **Don't drop price to $29-39** - Signals "commodity" not "specialist"
2. ❌ **Don't add $59 mid-tier** - Creates decision paralysis (too many options)
3. ❌ **Don't go monthly-only** - $14.99/month = $180/year (too expensive)
4. ❌ **Don't price match Wealthsimple** - They're Canada-only, different category
5. ❌ **Don't rush to $99** - Prove value first, raise price later
