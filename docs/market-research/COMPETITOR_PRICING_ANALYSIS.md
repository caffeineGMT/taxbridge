## 2. Detailed Competitor Analysis

### 2.1 Wealthsimple Tax (formerly SimpleTax)
**Pricing Model:** Pay-what-you-want with premium tiers

**Tiers:**
- **Basic:** $0 or any amount - 2 returns/year, all features
- **Plus:** $40 CAD (~$30 USD) - 8 returns/year, priority support, audit protection
- **Pro:** $80 CAD (~$59 USD) - 9+ returns, 30-min expert consultations

**Features:**
- 400+ tax credits/deductions search
- CRA auto-import
- Foreign income support
- Maximum refund guarantee

**Strengths:**
- Extremely affordable Canadian option
- No feature gating on free tier
- Strong brand (Wealthsimple backing)

**Weaknesses:**
- Canada-only focus
- No cross-border US/Canada specialization
- No RSU-specific tooling
- No FTC optimization

**Positioning vs TaxBridge:**
- Wealthsimple is a **general Canadian tax tool**, not specialized for cross-border
- TaxBridge offers **dual-country calculations + FTC optimizer** - higher value proposition
- Our $49-$79 pricing justified by specialization

---

### 2.2 TurboTax
**Pricing Model:** Tiered by tax complexity

**Tiers:**
- **Free:** $0 - Simple W-2 returns only (~37% of users)
- **Deluxe:** $59-$89 - Homeowners, deductions, donations
- **Premier:** $89-$149 - **Investments, RSUs, stock options** ← Closest competitor
- **Self-Employed:** $129-$209 - Business income, contractors
- **Expert Assist:** $89-$209 - On-demand expert help
- **Full Service:** $200+ - Expert files for you

**Features (Premier tier):**
- Investment income calculations
- Stock option and RSU reporting
- Capital gains/losses
- Rental property
- No cross-border specialization

**Strengths:**
- Market leader, trusted brand
- Comprehensive US tax coverage
- Expert support options

**Weaknesses:**
- **No Canada tax filing** - US only
- Generic RSU handling (no cross-border optimization)
- No Foreign Tax Credit optimizer for Canada
- State filing costs extra ($49-$59 each)
- Total cost for cross-border user: $149 (Premier) + $49 (state) = **$198 for US only**

**Positioning vs TaxBridge:**
- TurboTax Premier ($89-$149) handles US RSU taxes but **does NOT solve cross-border problem**
- User would need TurboTax ($149) + separate Canadian software ($40-$80) = **$189-$229 total**
- TaxBridge at **$49-$79 solves both countries** - **60-75% cost savings**
- TaxBridge offers **significantly better value** for dual filers

**Features:**
- Form 1040-NR (non-resident alien)
- Form 8843 (exempt individual)
- Form W-7 (ITIN application)
- Live tax professional support
- F-1/J-1/H-1B/L-1 visa support

**Strengths:**
- Simple, transparent pricing
- Non-resident specialization
- Good for international students
- Live support included

**Weaknesses:**
- **US federal only** - no state, no Canada
- Non-resident focus (not resident aliens)
- No RSU-specific tools
- No FTC optimization
- No multi-year tracking

**Positioning vs TaxBridge:**
- Glacier serves **non-resident aliens** (first 5 years on F-1/J-1)
- TaxBridge serves **resident aliens** on H-1B/TN with RSUs
- Different market segment, minimal overlap
- Our $49 pricing **matches** their flat rate, signals value

## 4. Pricing Positioning Analysis

### 4.1 TaxBridge Current Pricing
- **Free:** $0 - Limited RSU entries (3 or 5 depending on A/B test)
- **Pro Annual (Launch Special):** $49/year - Unlimited RSUs, FTC optimizer, PDF export, AI advisor
- **Pro Annual (Standard):** $79/year - Same features as launch special
- **Pro Monthly:** $14.99/month ($179.88/year) - Same features
- **Enterprise:** $2,000/year - Multi-client, white-label, API access

### 4.2 Market Position

**TaxBridge sits in the "specialist premium" category:**

```
COMMODITY (<$50)          SPECIALIST ($50-$100)     PREMIUM ($100-$200)        ENTERPRISE ($200+)
├─ Wealthsimple ($0-$59)  ├─ TaxBridge ($49-$79)   ├─ TurboTax Premier ($149)  ├─ TurboTax Full Service ($200+)
├─ H&R Block Free ($0)    ├─ Glacier ($49)         ├─ H&R Block Premium ($115)  ├─ TaxBridge Enterprise ($2,000)
                          ├─ Sprintax ($50-$75)    ├─ Sprintax Resident ($120)
```

**Value Proposition:**
- **70-80% cheaper** than using TurboTax + Wealthsimple separately ($189-$229)
- **Same price** as US-only specialist (Glacier $49)
- **Higher value** due to dual-country + FTC optimization

### 4.3 Price Sensitivity Analysis

**Willingness to Pay (estimated):**
- **H-1B workers at FAANG:** High income ($150k-$400k), will pay $100-$200 for accuracy
- **TN visa workers:** Moderate income ($80k-$150k), price-sensitive around $50-$100
- **First-year filers:** Very price-sensitive, need to "see it work" first
- **CPAs/Accountants:** Budget-constrained, evaluate ROI on client time saved

**Optimal Price Point:**
- **$49-$79 annual** hits sweet spot for majority
- **$49 launch special** effective for acquisition (anchored to Glacier)
- **$79 standard** justified by dual-country value vs $189-$229 alternative
- **$14.99 monthly** too expensive ($180/year), but serves "pay-as-you-go" preference

## 5. Competitive Threats & Opportunities

### 5.1 Threats
1. **TurboTax adds Canada filing:** Low risk (International expansion unlikely due to Intuit's US focus)
2. **Wealthsimple adds US filing:** Medium risk (They have US expansion ambitions, but complex regulatory barriers)
3. **Sprintax adds Canada:** Medium risk (Natural adjacent market, but would need CRA integration)
4. **New cross-border entrant:** Low-Medium risk (High barrier to entry: dual tax code, CRA/IRS integration, treaty knowledge)

### 5.2 Opportunities
1. **Increase Free tier limits (3→5 RSUs):** Currently A/B testing - data will inform decision
2. **Bundle with immigration services:** Partner with immigration lawyers ($200-$500 referral fees)
3. **Raise Pro price to $99:** Market can support it vs $189-$229 alternative, but risk churn
4. **Add "Pro Lite" tier at $29:** Capture ultra price-sensitive, may cannibalize $49 tier
5. **Enterprise upsell to accounting firms:** $2k/year with 20+ clients = $100/client (still profitable)

## 6. Pricing Recommendations

### ✅ RECOMMENDATION 1: MAINTAIN CURRENT PRO PRICING
**Keep $49 launch special through March 31, then $79 standard**

**Rationale:**
- $49 is **competitively aligned** with Glacier ($49) and Sprintax non-resident ($50-$75)
- $79 is **significantly below** alternative of TurboTax + Wealthsimple ($189-$229)
- Dual-country value justifies premium over single-country tools
- Pricing signals "specialist tool" not "commodity software"

**Evidence:**
- Glacier charges $49 for **US federal only**
- We charge $49 for **US + Canada + FTC optimization** - superior value
- TurboTax Premier ($149) doesn't solve Canada problem
- Customer saves $110-$150 vs buying two separate tools

### ✅ RECOMMENDATION 2: ADJUST FREE TIER MESSAGING
**Emphasize value, not restrictions**

**Current:** "3 RSU entries" or "5 RSU entries" (A/B test)
**Recommended:**
- **Option A:** "Starter Plan: 5 RSU entries" (winner of A/B test)
- **Option B:** "Free Trial: Unlock full calculator, upgrade to save results"

**Rationale:**
- Wealthsimple offers full features for $0 (pay-what-you-want)
- Our free tier should demonstrate value before asking for payment
- "5 RSU entries" is generous enough to complete 1 tax year calculation
- Focus on "upgrade to unlock PDF export + multi-year" not "unlock calculator"

### ✅ RECOMMENDATION 3: TEST $99 PRO TIER (Q2 2026)
**A/B test $99/year tier after proving product-market fit**

**Hypothesis:** Market can support $99 given savings vs alternatives

**Test Design:**
- **Control:** $79/year (current standard price)
- **Variant:** $99/year with "Advanced FTC Strategies" feature added
- **Success Metric:** <15% conversion rate drop, >25% revenue increase

**Go/No-Go Decision:**
- ✅ **GO** if paying customers >100 and churn <10%
- ❌ **NO-GO** if conversion drops >20% or churn spikes

**Rationale:**
- $99 is still **47% cheaper** than TurboTax + Wealthsimple ($189)
- Anchoring against $149 TurboTax Premier makes $99 feel reasonable
- Can justify with "Advanced" features: carryforward tracking, what-if scenarios, CPA-grade reports

### ❌ RECOMMENDATION 4: DO NOT ADD MID-TIER ($59)
**Avoid "goldilocks" pricing confusion**

**Risk:** Three annual tiers ($49/$59/$79) creates decision paralysis
**Better:** Two clear options - $49 launch special (time-limited) OR $79 standard (ongoing)

**Exception:** IF/WHEN we have distinct feature sets, THEN add tiers:
- **Basic $49:** Single tax year, no AI advisor
- **Pro $79:** Multi-year, AI advisor, priority support
- **Premium $129:** CPA review, audit protection, tax planning

But current feature set doesn't justify 3+ tiers yet.

### ✅ RECOMMENDATION 5: MAINTAIN ENTERPRISE PRICING ($2,000/year)
**Keep Enterprise at $2,000 with volume discount tiers**

**Current Structure:**
- **Enterprise:** $2,000/year - Up to 50 clients, white-label, API

**Recommended Structure:**
- **Enterprise Starter:** $2,000/year - Up to 20 clients ($100/client)
- **Enterprise Growth:** $4,000/year - Up to 60 clients ($67/client)
- **Enterprise Elite:** $8,000/year - Up to 150 clients ($53/client)
- **Enterprise Custom:** Quote-based - 150+ clients

**Rationale:**
- CPAs charge clients $500-$2,000 for cross-border tax prep
- If our tool saves 2-3 hours per client, CPA saves $200-$600 in labor
- ROI is clear at 10+ cross-border clients per year
- $2,000 is **affordable** for solo CPAs, **steal** for firms with 20+ clients
