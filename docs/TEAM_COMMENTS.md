## Team Member Instructions

**Before launch:**
1. Create/verify your Product Hunt account (must exist for 30+ days ideally)
2. Add a profile photo and bio (looks more credible)
3. Set phone alarm for your assigned comment time
4. Copy your comment to clipboard 5 min before posting
5. Join team Discord for coordination

**When posting:**
1. Wait for exact time (staggering prevents spam detection)
2. Paste your pre-written comment
3. Add 1-2 personal touches (make it authentic, not robotic)
4. Upvote the product (if you haven't already)
5. Report in Discord: "Comment #X posted ✅"

**After posting:**
1. Monitor your comment for replies
2. Engage with other comments (upvote good ones)
3. Share PH link on your social media (optional but helps)

## Don'ts

❌ Don't copy-paste word-for-word (add personal touches)
❌ Don't post all comments at once (looks fake)
❌ Don't mention "upvote this" explicitly (violates PH rules)
❌ Don't use team member accounts <30 days old (flagged as spam)
❌ Don't argue with negative comments (stay professional)
