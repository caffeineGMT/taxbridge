**Tools for screenshots:**
- macOS: Cmd+Shift+4 (select area) or Cmd+Shift+5 (full screen)
- Windows: Win+Shift+S or Snipping Tool
- Chrome DevTools: Device toolbar for mobile views
- CleanShot X (paid): https://cleanshot.com
- Annotate: Skitch, Snagit, or Markup (built-in macOS)

### 3. Product Demo Video (REQUIRED - 60-90 seconds)

Product Hunt requires or strongly recommends a video. This is your #1 conversion tool.

**Video structure (90 seconds max):**

**0:00-0:10 (Hook):**
- Open with the pain point: "Filing cross-border taxes costs $500-2K and takes weeks."
- Show frustrated user staring at tax forms

**0:10-0:20 (Solution intro):**
- "TaxBridge automates Foreign Tax Credit calculations in 5 minutes."
- Show TaxBridge logo + tagline

**0:20-0:60 (Product walkthrough):**
- Show key user flow:
  1. User lands on calculator (0:20-0:25)
  2. Inputs US income (W-2, RSUs) (0:25-0:35)
  3. Inputs Canadian income (T4) (0:35-0:45)
  4. Clicks "Calculate" → instant results (0:45-0:55)
  5. Shows tax savings breakdown (0:55-1:00)

**1:00-1:10 (Social proof):**
- Show testimonial quote: "Saved me $6,300 in taxes!" - John D., H-1B worker

**1:10-1:20 (Pricing + CTA):**
- "Just $49/year. Try the free calculator now."
- Show URL: taxbridge.app

**1:20-1:30 (Close):**
- "Launch special: 50% off with code PRODUCTHUNT50"
- End screen with logo + URL

**Video best practices:**
- **Keep it SHORT:** 60-90 seconds max (PH users have short attention)
- **No talking head:** Screen recording > founder talking (product-first)
- **Add captions:** Many watch with sound off
- **Fast-paced:** Quick cuts, no dead air
- **Music:** Upbeat background music (royalty-free from Epidemic Sound)

**Tools for video creation:**
- Screen recording: Loom, QuickTime (macOS), OBS Studio (free)
- Editing: iMovie (free), DaVinci Resolve (free), Adobe Premiere Pro (paid)
- Captions: Descript (auto-generates captions), Kapwing
- Music: Epidemic Sound, Artlist, YouTube Audio Library (free)

**Video specs:**
- **Resolution:** 1920x1080px (1080p)
- **Format:** MP4 or MOV
- **File size:** <100 MB
- **Length:** 60-90 seconds (PH recommends <2 min)

**File location:** `/launch/product-hunt/assets/demo-video.mp4`

### 4. Tagline (REQUIRED - <60 characters)

The tagline appears under your product name on Product Hunt. It's the FIRST thing people read.

**Formula:** [What it does] for [who it's for]

**Examples:**
- "Cross-border tax calculator for H-1B/TN workers" (49 chars) ✅
- "Automate Foreign Tax Credits for Canada workers" (48 chars) ✅
- "Save $3-7K/year on cross-border taxes" (38 chars) ✅
- "Tax filing made simple for H-1B workers in Canada" (50 chars) ✅

**Best practices:**
- Be specific (not "Tax software" but "Cross-border tax calculator")
- Include target audience (H-1B/TN workers)
- Avoid jargon (Foreign Tax Credit might be too technical)
- Test readability (show to 5 people, see if they get it)

### 5. Description (REQUIRED - 260 characters)

This appears on your Product Hunt page. Expand on the tagline.

**Formula:**
1. Pain point (1 sentence)
2. Solution (1 sentence)
3. Key benefit (1 sentence)

**Example (238 characters):**
> "Filing cross-border taxes between the US and Canada is a nightmare. TaxBridge automates Foreign Tax Credit calculations in 5 minutes. H-1B and TN workers save $3-7K/year vs. paying double taxes or hiring expensive CPAs. Try it free!"

**Best practices:**
- Start with pain point (hook empathy)
- Explain what it does (not how it works)
- Include key metric ($3-7K savings)
- End with CTA ("Try it free!")
