# Product Hunt First Comment Template

## Why the First Comment Matters

The **first comment** on your Product Hunt launch is critical:
- Sets the tone for the discussion
- Tells your founder story (builds trust)
- Explains unique value (converts browsers to users)
- Includes special offer (drives signups)
- Appears at the top (most visible)

**Best practices:**
- Post within 60 seconds of going live
- 200-300 words (not too long)
- Personal and authentic (not salesy)
- Include a special offer for PH community
- End with a question (drives engagement)

## Tips for Your First Comment

1. **Write it in advance** - you'll be too stressed on launch day
2. **Keep it authentic** - avoid corporate speak, be human
3. **Include your Twitter handle** - helps people connect with you
4. **Add a special offer** - PH users love exclusive deals (50% off is standard)
5. **End with a question** - drives more comments
6. **Pin it** - Product Hunt automatically pins the first comment from the maker

**Character limit:** No limit, but aim for 200-400 words. Longer = more scrolling = less engagement.

**Timing:** Post within **60 seconds** of launch. Set a timer.

**Tone:** Excited but not desperate. Humble but confident.
