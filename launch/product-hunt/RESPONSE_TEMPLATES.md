## Pro Tips for Launch Day Responses

### Speed Matters
- Reply within **10 minutes** to every comment
- VIP comments (investors, influencers) → **5 minutes**
- Use templates as a base, but personalize each reply

### Tone
- **Grateful, not desperate** ("Thanks for checking us out!" not "Please try it!")
- **Confident, not arrogant** ("We built this to solve X" not "We're the best")
- **Helpful, not salesy** (Educate first, convert second)

### Emojis
- Use sparingly (1-2 per comment max)
- 🙏 (thank you), 🎉 (celebration), 🚀 (launch day), ✅ (checklist)
- Avoid overuse (looks unprofessional)

### Engagement Tactics
- **End with a question** (drives more replies)
- **Tag people** if they asked a question earlier
- **Upvote every comment** (shows you're active)

### What NOT to Do
- ❌ Ignore criticism (respond diplomatically)
- ❌ Argue with trolls (take it to DM)
- ❌ Copy-paste same reply (looks lazy)
- ❌ Go silent for hours (kills momentum)

---

**Launch Day Mantra:**
Reply fast. Be helpful. Stay positive. Convert later.

Good luck! 🚀
