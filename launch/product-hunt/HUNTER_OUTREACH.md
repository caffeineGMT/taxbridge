# Product Hunt Hunter Outreach Templates

## What is a "Hunter"?

A **hunter** is a Product Hunt user who submits your product on your behalf. Benefits:
- Hunters with large followings bring built-in upvotes
- Established hunters signal credibility
- Their network is already engaged on PH

**Strategy:** Find hunters with audiences in tax/finance/immigration/H-1B niches.

## Ideal Hunter Profile

✅ **Good Fit:**
- Audience: tax professionals, CPAs, immigration lawyers, H-1B workers
- PH reputation: 1,000+ followers, active commenter
- Niche: finance, SaaS, legal tech, productivity tools
- Engagement: replies to comments, participates in discussions

❌ **Avoid:**
- Generic hunters with no relevant audience
- Pay-to-hunt services (against PH rules)
- Inactive accounts (no recent submissions)

## Backup Plan (If No Hunter Commits)

**Self-Hunt Strategy:**
If you can't secure a hunter by March 23, you can submit yourself:

1. **Create a team account** (not personal) on Product Hunt
2. **Ask a friend with a PH account** to submit (even if they're not a "hunter")
3. **Focus on organic growth** (email list + supporter mobilization)
4. **Leverage your own network** (Twitter, LinkedIn followers)

**Note:** Hunters are helpful but NOT required. Many successful launches are self-hunted.
