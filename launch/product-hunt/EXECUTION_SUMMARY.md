## 🎯 Launch Strategy Overview

### Pre-Launch (March 19-24)
**Goal:** Build anticipation and mobilize supporters

**Key Activities:**
1. **Hunter Outreach (March 20-22):** Identify and secure committed hunter with relevant audience (tax/finance/immigration niche)
2. **Asset Creation (March 19-23):** Prepare logo, gallery screenshots, demo video, tagline, description, first comment
3. **Community Pre-Seeding (March 20-24):** 5-day social media countdown on Twitter/LinkedIn, email teaser to 500+ subscribers, Reddit posts in r/h1b and r/ImmigrationCanada
4. **Influencer Outreach (March 20-22):** DM 20 tax CPAs, H-1B YouTubers, immigration lawyers for launch day amplification
5. **Supporter Mobilization (March 23-24):** Compile list of 50 supporters (friends, beta users, advisors), create WhatsApp coordination group, send easy comment starters

### Launch Day (March 25, 12:01 AM - 11:59 PM PT)
**Goal:** #1 Product of the Day

**Shift Schedule:**
- **Shift 1 (12:01 AM - 6:00 AM):** Submit product, post first comment, monitor approval
- **Shift 2 (6:00 AM - 12:00 PM):** Morning blitz (email blast, social media, Reddit posts, activate paid ads)
- **Shift 3 (12:00 PM - 6:00 PM):** Maintain engagement velocity, reply to every comment <10 min, monitor rankings
- **Shift 4 (6:00 PM - 11:59 PM):** Final push, thank supporters, capture metrics

**Response Protocol:**
- VIP comments: <5 min reply
- Questions: <10 min reply
- Criticism: <15 min reply (diplomatic)
- All comments: upvote + personalized response (no copy-paste)

### Post-Launch (March 26+)
**Goal:** Convert traffic and build momentum

**Day 1 Activities:**
- Results post on Twitter/LinkedIn with metrics
- Thank you email to supporters + new signups
- Add Product Hunt badge to website homepage
- Press outreach to TechCrunch, immigration blogs

**Week 1 Activities:**
- Email drip campaign for new trial users
- Implement top feature requests from PH community
- Analytics review (conversion rates, traffic sources)
- Paid retargeting for PH visitors who didn't convert

## 🚨 Critical Success Factors

### 1. Hunter Commitment (Due: March 23)
**Why It Matters:** Hunters with large followings bring built-in upvotes and signal credibility

**Action Items:**
- Send outreach to 10-15 hunters (March 20-21)
- Follow up March 22
- Secure commitment by March 23
- Backup: self-hunt if no hunter commits

### 2. Asset Quality (Due: March 24 6 PM PT)
**Why It Matters:** First impressions drive upvotes and conversion

**Required Assets:**
- Logo: 240x240px PNG, transparent background
- Gallery: 5-8 screenshots (1920x1080px)
- Video: 60-90 sec demo (1080p, <100 MB)
- Copy: tagline (<60 chars), description (260 chars), first comment (200-400 words)

### 3. Supporter Mobilization (Due: March 24)
**Why It Matters:** First hour velocity determines ranking

**Action Items:**
- Compile list of 50 supporters (friends, beta users, advisors)
- Create WhatsApp coordination group
- Send "easy comment starters" (so they don't have to think)
- Alert at 12:01 AM PT when product goes live

### 4. Fast Response Time (Launch Day)
**Why It Matters:** Engagement velocity drives rankings

**Protocol:**
- Reply to every comment within 10 minutes (VIPs within 5 min)
- Use pre-written templates from RESPONSE_TEMPLATES.md
- Personalize each response (no copy-paste)
- Upvote every comment

### 5. CMO Coordination (Due: March 21)
**Why It Matters:** CMO owns community engagement and paid promotion

**Deliverables:**
- Approve $2,000 paid promo budget
- Execute 5-day social media countdown (March 20-24)
- Send influencer outreach (20 DMs)
- Activate paid ads on launch day (6 AM PT)
- Monitor PH comments all day (<10 min reply SLA)
