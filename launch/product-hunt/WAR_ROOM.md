## Response Protocols

### Comment Reply SLA
- **VIP comments (investors, influencers):** <5 minutes
- **Questions:** <10 minutes
- **General praise:** <20 minutes
- **Criticism:** <15 minutes (use diplomatic template)

### Crisis Management
**If product drops below Top 5 by noon:**
1. Activate backup supporter list (10-15 people on standby)
2. Increase paid Twitter promotion budget
3. Post urgent appeal on LinkedIn
4. DM top engaged users to share with their networks

**If negative comments appear:**
1. Respond within 5 minutes with empathy
2. Offer to fix issue immediately
3. Take conversation to DM if heated
4. Do NOT argue publicly

**If technical issues (site down, payment broken):**
1. Alert engineering immediately
2. Post transparent update on PH ("We're aware, fixing now")
3. Estimate fix time publicly
4. Post resolved update when fixed
